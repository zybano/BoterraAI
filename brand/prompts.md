Palette to reference in every prompt: midnight navy #0A1230, signal blue #2B5CFF, electric violet #7B5CFF, pulse teal #14E0C0, silver #B9C1D3.
Rules: no robots or faces on machines, always midnight navy as the base, one gradient accent, generous negative space, add real text in design tools unless the generator is Ideogram or DALL·E.

## Midjourney (v7)

Midjourney is strongest for mood, backgrounds, 3D renders and photography. It still garbles text, so add headlines afterwards in Figma. Use --style raw for brand-accurate colour, and once you like an output, reuse it with --sref to keep the series consistent.

## Stable Diffusion (SDXL / SD 3.5 / FLUX)

Use for backgrounds, textures and 3D concepts you can iterate on locally. Suggested settings: 1344×768 (16:9) or 1024×1024, 30 steps, CFG 5–6 (FLUX: guidance 3.5), DPM++ 2M Karras. Vectorise any logo explorations by hand: never ship a raster logo.
